# Five-market depth

## Why
- **The owner's ask (3 October 2026):** other markets are shallow next to India's 500 companies, and India itself should reach 95% coverage.
- **What it unlocks:** the five-market data stories, and the [culture test](../research/culture-and-parity-2026-10.md), which needs 20 or more countries to mean anything.

## Decisions (owner, 3 October 2026)
- **Indexes:** FTSE 350, ASX 300, TOPIX 500 and the STOXX Europe 600 members listed in EU countries, each beside the existing flagship index.
- **India's gaps:** from companies' own annual reports, as NSE downloads are on hold ([legal review](../reviews/legal-review-2026-10.md)).
- **Estimates count** toward India's 95%, badged as today. Reported figures alone top out near 89%.
- **Run now, solo,** in this order: Japan, UK, Australia, India, EU.

## India: what is left (3 October 2026)
- **Read from annual reports (22):** HYUNDAI, VBL, ADANIGREEN, GAIL, MFSL, UNOMINDA, BAJAJHLDNG, TITAGARH, DEVYANI, IGL, HEXT, ABB, CASTROLIND, CRISIL, HONASA, ANANDRATHI, AIIL, SYNGENE, ABBOTINDIA, HBLENGINE, CYIENT, plus FSL (left out: 53.3% against 33.6% a year earlier).
- **Left out on purpose:** GILLETTE (49.6% after 12 to 15%, same staff mix) and FSL, both under the 15-point jump rule.
- **Found only on NSE's archive (about 15):** INDIGO, BPCL, HINDPETRO, IEX, TEJASNET, OLAELEC, MMTC, ADANIENSOL, SCHAEFFLER, KSB, CIEINDIA, CENTRALBK, CEMPRO, ITI, MCX. Their investor pages build links in the browser, so the PDFs cannot be found on their own sites by script.
- **Structurally empty:** three REITs (BAGMANE, BIRET, EMBASSY) and DUMMYHEG file no BRSR; most 2025-26 listings (Pine Labs, Ather and others) published no BRSR in their first annual report.

- Shared measures and the 30% to 130% wage parity filter as in [decision 0001](../decisions/0001-comparable-parity-measures.md).

- Japan moves to TSE 33-sector labels for every company, from JPX's listing file, so the Nikkei 225 and TOPIX 500 share one scheme.
