# Ship-readiness UX audit, 2 October 2026

Audited build: `main` at da04b87, live at nivel-equal-lens.vercel.app. Design system: [../design.md](../design.md). **Status:** blockers B1 to B6 and most majors were fixed in PARITY-24; see [After the fixes](#after-the-fixes-parity-24). B7 waits on a corrections address.

## Verdict

| Release | Ready? | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| **Guided demo** (MIT Solve pitch, investor walkthroughs) | **Yes** | Nothing blocking. Avoid the Companies search empty state and the phone search sheet on stage. |
| **Public beta** (link shared openly) | **No** | Fix the 7 blockers below. About 2 to 3 days of work, plus a corrections address from the team. |
| **Accessible launch** (WCAG 2.2 AA) | **No** | Blockers B1 to B3 plus majors M3 and M4. |

The product's core is strong. Numbers trace to filings, data quality is labelled honestly, every market shares one headline scale, and phone lists now follow platform conventions. What holds it back is accessibility basics (contrast, page titles, skip link, target sizes), two navigation bugs (state lost on Back, tab bar over the search sheet) and the missing corrections channel.

## How it was tested

- **Viewports:** Pixel 9 width (412 × 915), 320px (the WCAG reflow width) and desktop (1280 × 900), light and dark.
- **Routes:** 20, covering home, four index pages, the directory, five company tabs, companies from every market, Compare's three tabs, Method and a 404.
- **Automated checks** ([assets/ux-audit-checks.html](assets/ux-audit-checks.html)):
  - computed text contrast against the real background
  - interactive element sizes (24px, 44pt and 48dp thresholds)
  - accessible names, heading order, sideways overflow, page titles, skip link and live regions
  - raw results in `assets/ux-audit-2026-10-02-*.json`
  - to rerun, copy the file into `dist/` after `pnpm build`, serve with `pnpm preview`, and open `/ux-audit-checks.html?w=412&theme=light` with Chrome's `--dump-dom`
  - run one viewport at a time, each with a fresh `--user-data-dir` and `--virtual-time-budget=150000`. Parallel runs stall, and macOS has no `timeout` command, so use a shell loop as the watchdog. Headless windows are at least 500px wide; the checker sizes its own frames, so this does not affect results.
- **Task walkthroughs:**
  1. find a company's share of pay
  2. compare two markets
  3. see how a number was calculated
  4. browse a long index list and come back
  5. search for something that does not exist
  6. install the app
- **Evidence:** screenshots in [assets/](assets/) (`ux-audit-2026-10-02-*.jpg`).

## Blockers (fix before a public beta)

**B1. Light-mode text fails contrast (WCAG 1.4.3, AA).**
- **Problem:** `--muted` #9286AE reads 3.36:1 on cards, 3.17:1 on the page background and 2.99:1 on tint. It carries subtitles, axis labels, tile notes, flat changes and the ⌘K hint.
- **Also failing:**
  - `--soft` on tint: 4.35:1, used for table headers and "no change"
  - the Estimated badge: 3.06:1
  - the Missing badge: 4.01:1
  - the Limited data status: 3.44:1
- **Scale:** the checker counted 1,056 failing text nodes on phone and 2,243 on desktop, across all 20 routes. Dark mode passes everywhere.
- **Fix:** change the light tokens only.
  - `--muted` to #736893 (5.1 / 4.8 / 4.5 on card, paper, tint)
  - `--soft` to #6E5695 (6.1 / 5.8 / 5.5)
  - `--sun-deep` to #8A5D00 (5.8 / 5.4 / 5.1)
  - `--bad` to #A9442F (5.9 / 5.6 / 5.3)
- **Effort:** about an hour, then rerun the checker.

**B2. Every page has the same title, and navigation is silent to screen readers (WCAG 2.4.2 and 4.1.3, A and AA).**
- **Problem:** all 20 routes are titled "Nivel · The Equal Lens", so browser tabs, history and bookmarks are indistinguishable. With hash routing, the page changes but nothing is announced. Focus stays on a link that no longer exists, so it falls back to the start of the page.
- **Fix:**
  - set `document.title` per route (for example "TCS · Calculations · Nivel")
  - move focus to the page's `h1` after navigation
  - announce the new title in a visually hidden live region
- **Effort:** about 2 hours.

**B3. No skip link (WCAG 2.4.1, A).**
- **Problem:** keyboard users pass about 14 stops on every page (brand, four nav links, search, theme, seven strip items) before reaching content.
- **Fix:** a "Skip to content" link as the first focusable element, targeting `main`.
- **Effort:** 15 minutes.

**B4. The bottom tab bar paints over the search sheet (bug).**
- **Problem:** the sheet is rendered inside the sticky header, whose stacking context sits at z-index 20, below the tab bar at 25. The tab bar shows on top of the open sheet (evidence: `ux-audit-2026-10-02-sheet-overlap.jpg`). Tapping a tab navigates away with the sheet still open and the page scroll-locked.
- **Fix:** render the sheet in a portal on `document.body`.
- **Effort:** 30 minutes.

**B5. Lists forget where you were (Nielsen H3, platform navigation norms).**
- **Problem:**
  - the router scrolls to the top on every hash change, including Back
  - the Companies index filter and every table's sort live in component state
  - so after reading a company on the Nikkei list and pressing Back, the filter returns to Nifty 50, the sort to default and the page to the top
  - this breaks the core browse-and-inspect loop on long lists (225 Nikkei rows)
- **Fix:**
  - scroll to the top only on new navigation, and restore scroll on Back and Forward
  - keep the filter and sort in the URL (`#/companies?index=all&sort=e`) so they survive Back and can be shared
- **Effort:** about half a day.

**B6. Touch targets are below platform minimums (Apple HIG 44pt, Material 48dp; WCAG 2.5.8 in places).**
- **Problem:** on a 412px phone, 85 to 98% of interactive elements are under 44px:
  - filter pills: 30px
  - dropdowns: 32px
  - segmented buttons: about 29px
  - sort, direction and CSV buttons: 30 to 34px
  - section tabs: about 40px
  - header icons: 40px
- **WCAG 2.5.8 (24px minimum):** a few standalone links fail on phones (XBRL and PDF at 22px tall; "See it on the parity map" at 22px). On desktop, table sort buttons are 16px tall.
- **Fix:** give every control a 44px hit area on phones with padding or an invisible hit slop, keeping the visual size. Give standalone links a full-height row.
- **Effort:** about half a day.

**B7. No way to report an error or reach the team (Nielsen H9 and H10, trust).**
- **Problem:** the copy says "tell The Equal Lens team" in four places, with no link or address (Plane PARITY-7 is still open). A product that names companies and ranks them needs a visible corrections route before it is shared widely. This is a reputational and legal safeguard as much as a UX one.
- **Fix:** a "Report a correction" link (mailto or form) in the footer, on Method and in every company's Sources tab, plus the date each figure was last checked.
- **Effort:** about an hour once the team gives an address.

## Major (fix in the first week after beta)

**M1. One concept, many names (Nielsen H4 consistency, H2 match with the real world).** The three shared measures appear under about a dozen labels:
- **Share of pay:**
  - "Women's share of the payroll you own"
  - "Women's share of wages"
  - "Women's share of pay"
  - "Implied share of pay"
  - "Headline share of pay"
  - "Pay to women"
- **Employment parity:**
  - "Women in permanent staff"
  - "Women in workforce"
  - "Women in UK workforce"
  - "Women in regular staff"
  - "Employment parity"
- **Wage parity:**
  - "Women's average pay"
  - "Women's pay per 100 of men's"
  - "Pay per 100 of men's"
  - "Wage parity"

Readers have to work out that these are the same thing.
- **Fix:** one label per concept from a single glossary module, with each market's basis as a subtitle. For example, "Women's share of staff", subtitle "permanent staff".
- **Effort:** about a day, covering copy and code.

**M2. Dead-end empty states (Nielsen H9, H1).**
- **Problem:** a Companies search with no match shows "0 companies" over an empty card, with no message and no way back. The desktop header search shows nothing at all; the phone sheet does say "No matches".
- **Fix:** an empty state that repeats the query, offers a Clear button and suggests trying a ticker or sector.
- **Effort:** about an hour.

**M3. Pages scroll sideways at 320px (WCAG 1.4.10 Reflow, AA).**

| Route | Overflow | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Nifty 50, FTSE 100 and Euro Stoxx 50 index pages | 82px | The "Biggest moves" tables keep a 180px minimum name column |
| Sectors | 66px | The three-option measure switch can't shrink |
| TCS Trend | 9px | Line charts have a 300px minimum width |

- **Fix:** remove the minimum width in small tables, let the switch scroll, and use a 260px chart minimum.
- **Effort:** about an hour.

**M4. The active tab-bar item is shown by colour alone (WCAG 1.4.1; Material 3 navigation bar).**
- **Problem:** the active item differs only by violet against grey-violet.
- **Fix:** the Material active-indicator pill behind the icon, or a filled icon with a bold label.
- **Effort:** 30 minutes.

**M5. The theme button hides its state (Nielsen H1, H6).**
- **Problem:** one icon cycles system, light and dark with no visible label. On touch there is no tooltip, so people cannot tell the current or next state.
- **Fix:** a small menu (System, Light, Dark) with a check on the current choice.
- **Effort:** about an hour.

**M6. Shared links have no preview, and pages can't be indexed.**
- **Problem:** there are no Open Graph or Twitter tags, so WhatsApp, LinkedIn and X show a bare link. Hash routes are not indexed and every page shares one title. For an awareness product, sharing is the main way people will find it.
- **Fix:** Open Graph and Twitter tags with a share image (about an hour). Later, path-based routes with prerendered index and company pages (2 to 3 days).

**M7. No error boundary.**
- **Problem:** any runtime error blanks the whole app with no way to recover.
- **Fix:** a top-level error boundary with a short apology and Reload and Home links.
- **Effort:** 30 minutes.

**M8. Very long pages on phones (Nielsen H8, minimalist design).**
- **Problem:** an index page runs hero, trend, sectors, biggest moves and the full company list (up to 225 rows) with no in-page navigation.
- **Fix:** section tabs on index pages (Overview, Companies, Sectors), or a sticky "Jump to companies" chip. Show the first 20 companies with a "Show all" button.
- **Effort:** about half a day.

## Minor

**m1. Jargon without inline help (Nielsen H6).**
- **Problem:** "pp", "≈", "middle half", "percentile" and "like for like" are explained only on Method or in hover tooltips. Phones have no hover.
- **Fix:** a one-line key under list headers ("≈ estimated · pp = percentage points") and tap-to-explain info buttons on the scale cards.

**m2. Look-alike chips (Gestalt similarity).**
- **Problem:** three different kinds of chip look the same:
  - index chips on company pages are links styled like status pills
  - change chips (tinted pill with a triangle) look tappable but aren't
  - filter pills look like both
- **Fix:** give links, statuses and filters three distinct treatments.

**m3. Text under 12px (HIG body minimums, Material small labels).**
- **Problem:** axis labels and badges are 10 to 11px, the brand subtitle is 10.5px, plus the quadrant key. Many of these also fail contrast in light mode.
- **Fix:** a 12px floor on phones.

**m4. The index strip cuts off without a cue (Gestalt continuity).**
- **Problem:** at 1280px, "Euro Stoxx 50" is clipped at the right edge, with no fade or arrow to show the strip scrolls.
- **Fix:** fit all seven items on desktop, and add an edge fade on phones.

**m5. No offline indicator (Nielsen H1).**
- **Problem:** offline, the app quietly shows cached data.
- **Fix:** a slim "Offline. Showing data collected 1 October 2026" banner.

**m6. Charts waste space on phones.**
- **Problem:** line charts reserve 112px on the right for end labels even at 412px, which squeezes the plot.
- **Fix:** a smaller right margin on narrow charts.

**m7. Skipped heading levels (WCAG 1.3.1).**
- **Problem:** levels skip (h2 to h4) on Compare facets and the company Trend and Sources tabs.

**m8. The install prompt can interrupt reading (NN/g on interruptions).**
- **Problem:** it appears 8 seconds into the first visit.
- **Fix:** show it after a second page view or deep scroll instead.

**m9. Monograms look unlike logo tiles.**
- **Problem:** 80 companies (12%) show monograms, which differ visually from logo tiles.
- **Fix:** acceptable for now. A logo service key would close the gap.

**m10. One large JavaScript bundle.**
- **Problem:** 1.31 MB of JavaScript (217 KB gzipped) in one chunk, including all data. That is fine on a Pixel 9 over 4G, but about 3 seconds on slow 3G.
- **Fix:** split the data per market and lazy-load Compare.

## Nitpicks

- **Brand subtitle:** "by The Equal Lens" at 10.5px is near unreadable on phones.
- **404 page:** it offers only "Back to indexes"; add search.
- **Footer:** it is long on phones; the trademark sentence could move to Method.
- **Search placeholders:** they disagree ("Search companies and indexes" against "Search by name, symbol or sector").
- **Install popover:** "Not now" and the close button do the same thing; keep one.
- **Pace of change callout:** its five long paragraphs are dense on phones; a small table (market, median change, years to parity) would scan faster.
- **Page transitions:** route changes are abrupt. A short fade, skipped under reduced motion, would soften them (Material motion).

## What works well (keep)

- **Traceability.**
  - Every number links back to its filing (Sources) and is worked out step by step (Calculations).
  - That is rare and strong for trust (H10).
- **Honest labelling.**
  - Data-quality markers sit on the figures themselves: estimated (≈), not comparable, missing.
  - Excluded companies are listed with their reasons.
- **One scale.** Share of pay, with parity at 50%, works across all five markets, so indexes can sit side by side.
- **Phone lists.**
  - Two-line rows: the figure follows the sort, and the header names it.
  - No meters, labels or pills in the rows (HIG and Material list patterns).
- **Platform conventions.**
  - A bottom tab bar, a full-screen search sheet with Cancel, safe-area padding, an installable app with offline support, and system dark mode.
  - Dark mode passes contrast everywhere.
- **Keyboard and assistive tech.**
  - ⌘K search, visible focus rings on every custom control, and a fully keyboard-operable dropdown.
  - Sortable headers expose `aria-sort`.
  - Charts carry text summaries and a table view.
- **Gestalt.** Consistent card regions, right-aligned tabular numbers, labels kept close to their values, and the parity quadrant tinted as figure against ground.
