# Parity 50, methodology version 1

## Purpose and status
- **What it is:** a 50-stock version of the [Parity-Aligned Nifty 500](0006-parity-aligned-nifty-500.md). It represents the Indian large-cap market the way the Nifty 50 does, with as much parity as that allows.
- **Why 50 stocks:** small enough for a model portfolio such as a smallcase.
- **Illustrative only:** not investable as published here, not advice, not a rating.
- **Who could offer it:** a model portfolio must be published by a SEBI-registered research analyst, investment adviser, portfolio manager or broker. The Equal Lens would supply the data and the method, and a registered manager would own the recommendation.
- **Pre-registered:** committed on 2 October 2026 before any return of this portfolio was computed.

## Universe and the market it represents
- **Universe:** Nifty 100 members as on 30 September 2026. Survivorship bias is reported with the results.
- **The market it represents:** the Nifty 50 at free-float market weights, computed as in decision 0006. Nifty Next 50 members have a market weight of zero.
- **Rebalancing:** the last trading day of September each year. Backtest portfolios: 2024 and 2025. 2026 is the current composition.

## Data
- **Point in time,** as in decision 0006:
  - market capitalisation from NSE's file on the date;
  - free float from the latest shareholding pattern on or before 30 June;
  - the company's own BRSR for the fiscal year that ended that March, received by NSE by the date, with women's share of wages strictly between 0 and 1, and women's share of permanent staff reported.
- **Missing data:** companies without valid parity data are not held.

## Construction
- **Holdings:** exactly 50 companies.
- **Each holding:**
  - at least 0.5%;
  - for a Nifty 50 member, at most three times its market weight and never more than 10%;
  - for a Nifty Next 50 member, at most 3%.
- **Sectors:** each NSE industry within 2 points of its Nifty 50 weight.
- **Closeness to the market:** active share against the Nifty 50 at most 20%.
- **Objective:** the highest women's share of owned payroll these limits allow, solved as a mixed-integer programme. The weights are those of the least-active solution at that level.

## Returns and comparison
- **Total return:** month-end NSE closes, adjusted for splits and bonus issues, with dividends reinvested. Weights drift between rebalances.
- **Comparison:**
  - the Nifty 50 at market weights, from the same data;
  - for context, the SBI Nifty 50 ETF and the ICICI Prudential BSE Sensex ETF.
- **Reported:**
  - return, volatility, largest fall;
  - tracking error, beta and active return against the Nifty 50;
  - the parity figures each year;
  - the companies in and out against the Nifty 50.
- **Sensitivity checks:**
  - active share limits of 10% and 30%;
  - sector weights exactly the Nifty 50's, if feasible.

## Glide path and gap list
As in decision 0006:
- the portfolio aims to close a tenth of its remaining gap to 50% each year;
- it says where weighting cannot;
- it lists the companies holding the gap.

## Limits stated with the results
- **History:** two years.
- **Survivorship:** Nifty 100 membership of September 2026.
- **Data rules:** as for decision 0006.
- **Not advice:** past results do not predict future returns. A live model portfolio would follow its registered manager's obligations, including the rules on stating performance.

## Correction to the return calculation (2 October 2026, after the first run)
- **What changed:** total return now also values demerged shares, adjusts for rights issues, and follows renamed companies by ISIN. The text above is unchanged.
- **Why:** the first run counted a demerger as a fall in the parent's price and dropped companies that had changed symbol.
- **Reporting:** both runs are in the [results](../research/parity-50-results.md#correction-after-the-first-run). The same correction applies to decisions 0005 and 0006.
