# parity-tracker domain glossary

_Key terms and concepts in this project's domain. One entry per term; link from pages that use it._

- **Headline:** each index's figure: women's share of pay, parity 50%, in every market. India reports it; the UK and Australia derive it per employer; Japan and the EU derive it from E and W (since PARITY-20). Inputs differ, so small gaps between markets are noise.
- **Employment parity (E):** women's share of staff; parity 50%. Shared across markets. See [knowledge/decisions/0001](../../knowledge/decisions/0001-comparable-parity-measures.md).
- **Wage parity (W):** women's average pay as a share of men's; parity 100%. Shared across markets; India's is derived and approximate.
- **Implied share of pay (S):** E×W ÷ (E×W + 1 − E); parity 50%.
- **Market percentile:** a company's position on wage parity among companies in its own market; the fair way to rank across markets.
- **Like for like:** a year-on-year change that only counts companies reporting on the same basis in both years.
